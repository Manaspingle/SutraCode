Evidence-Grounded RAG Architecture

How SutraTrace anchors AI debugging in verified technical specifications instead of speculative generation.

1. Ingestion & Splitting

PyMuPDF parses PDF/TXT documents, tracking page numbers and slicing content into 700–1,000 character windows with 150-char overlap.

2. Local Vector Store

Passages are vectorized with all-MiniLM-L6-v2 (384 dims) and stored in ChromaDB with strict per-user metadata isolation.

3. Gemini Synthesis

Top cosine-similarity passages feed into Google Gemini under untrusted content constraints, eliminating prompt injection risks.

4. Verifiable Diffs

Generates a surgical, minimal line diff, verbatim citations with page references, and executable assertions for local testing.
